# Daily digest — 2026-10-27

> The throughline today is the gap between controlling systems and being accountable for them. Qwen's single-neuron persona flip is the interpretability version of this: a model can pass every benchmark and still sit one flipped unit away from an alternate persona, which means behavior-level evals never probe the mechanism underneath. Medicare's AI experiment is the deployment-side version — failures surfaced through FOIA'd oversight documents rather than pre-launch testing, and the GSA deal is about to put Claude inside that same accountability regime at national scale. The code review stat is the human mirror: owners raise reliability twice as often as reviewers because they're the ones who live with the failure modes everyone else gets to skip.
