# Daily digest — 2026-11-23

> Today's throughline is making agentic coding systems actually dependable: persistent sessions, scalable memory, deterministic guards, and tamper-evident retrieval all target continuity and trust rather than raw capability. At the same time, efficiency and infrastructure work—sparse distillation, quantized reward-model instability, MoE warp scheduling, and GPU optimization—shows the stack is being tuned for cost and throughput. Security and evaluation integrity recur across the digest, from encrypted vector search leakage and RAG poisoning defenses to benchmark gaming and markdown rules that fail to constrain agents. The practical takeaway is to treat agent deployments as systems problems: measure kernel-level performance, separate privileges in evaluation, and validate recovery paths before scaling.
