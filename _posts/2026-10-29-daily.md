# Daily digest — 2026-10-29

> Agentic coding went from novelty to default workflow this cycle: Linus Torvalds is running Claude on the Linux kernel, Kimi K3.1 Pro is benchmarking against Opus-class models on agentic tasks, and Cursor shipped an Agent SDK to formalize the pattern. But the friction is showing too — Claude Code was caught sending background agent transcripts to its model, a privacy leak worth checking your config for, and two separate takes on "brutal realities" suggest shipping agentic products is harder than the demos imply. Meanwhile the supply chain stays under attack: Apple's git still trusts compromised eMudhra certificates and GitLab disclosed an attempted exploit of CVE-2026-97229, so patch and audit your trust stores. The quiet signal: StackOverflow is down to under 1,000 questions a month — developers are asking agents instead of forums now.
